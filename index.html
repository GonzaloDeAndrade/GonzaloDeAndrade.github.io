About

Practical software for real problems

I’m a software developer from Argentina. My experience mixes two worlds: traditional web development (frontend, backend, databases and integrations) and business software, especially customization and automation inside the Zoho ecosystem.

I like understanding how a business actually works and then building the piece that is missing — a web app, an automation, or the integration that moves data between two systems that didn’t talk before. I adapt to the stack the project needs, and I care about things working end to end, not just in a demo.

Spanish is my native language and I work comfortably in English in professional and technical contexts.

On AI-assisted development

I use AI tools in my daily workflow: researching documentation, exploring implementation options, reviewing code, debugging and learning new technologies faster. They speed up my work; they don’t replace understanding it. In each project I focus on the decisions and code I can explain and defend.
